GIS = Geographic Information System. It organizes, analyzes, visualizes, and shares information linked to locations. Its key question is “Where?”

Knowing where helps us understand what, when, how, and why something happened, connecting our local environment to the wider world.

Five components: hardware, software, data, people, and methods/applications.

Hardware: physical equipment that runs software, stores collected data, and lets users interact with it.

  • Collection: GPS, cameras, probes.
  • Input: scanners, digitizers.
  • Output: printers, computer monitors, plotters.
  • Analysis and storage: computers, hard drives.

Applications: locating disease outbreaks, recording a new frog species’ location, tracking tornado paths, and finding the nearest supermarket.
